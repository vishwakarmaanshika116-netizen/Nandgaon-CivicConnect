Frequently Asked Questions

Find simple answers to common questions about Nandgaon CivicConnect, complaint registration, tracking and digital grievance services.

❓

How can we help you?

Explore the questions below to learn more about using CivicConnect and accessing digital civic services.

Go to the "Register Complaint" page, enter your required details, describe your issue and submit the complaint form.

A Complaint ID is a unique reference number provided for identifying and tracking your registered complaint.

Open the "Track Complaint" page and enter your Complaint ID to view the current status of your complaint.

CPGRAMS stands for Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System. It is an online platform for citizens to submit and track public grievances.

Yes. You can upload a relevant photo or video while registering your complaint. This can help explain the issue clearly.

e-Governance helps citizens access government services digitally and supports transparency, accessibility and efficient public service delivery.

Citizens should provide only the information required for registering a complaint and avoid sharing passwords, OTPs or other confidential information.

You can check the complaint status using your Complaint ID and, where appropriate, use the relevant government grievance redressal mechanism such as CPGRAMS.
