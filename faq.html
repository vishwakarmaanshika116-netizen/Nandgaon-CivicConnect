Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Nandgaon CivicConnect.

You can register a complaint by visiting the Complaint section of Nandgaon CivicConnect. Fill in your required details, select the complaint category and area, describe the issue and submit the complaint.

Citizens can report common local civic issues such as roads, drainage, garbage, water supply, street lights, sanitation and other public service-related problems.

Yes. Supporting photos or videos can help explain the civic issue more clearly and provide useful information for complaint verification and processing.

During complaint registration, select the appropriate ward or local area where the civic issue is located. This helps direct the complaint to the appropriate authority.

Yes. CivicConnect is designed to provide complaint tracking so that citizens can check the progress and current status of their submitted complaint.

After submission, the complaint can be reviewed and processed by the concerned authority. Its status can be updated as the issue moves through the complaint management process.

Accurate complaint information helps authorities understand the issue, identify its location and take appropriate action. Clear descriptions and supporting media can make the complaint easier to understand.

The platform is designed for local citizens, students, youth, shopkeepers, elderly residents and other community members who want to report and follow up on civic issues.

Nandgaon CivicConnect aims to make civic complaint registration easier, improve communication between citizens and authorities, and promote transparency and digital participation in local governance.

You can explore the Home, About, Services, Complaint and Feedback sections of Nandgaon CivicConnect for additional information and available platform features.
