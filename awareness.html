e-Governance Awareness

Learn how digital government services can make public services easier, faster and more accessible.

What is e-Governance?

e-Governance means using Information and Communication Technology to provide government services to citizens in a simple, transparent and efficient way.

Digital Government Services

💻 Online Services

Citizens can access many government services through digital platforms.

📝 Online Grievances

Citizens can submit complaints and grievances through online portals.

📱 Digital Access

Digital platforms allow citizens to access information from anywhere.

🔍 Transparency

Online tracking helps citizens know the status of their requests and complaints.

Why Digital Awareness is Important?

Stay Safe Online

Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs or other sensitive information with unknown persons.
